BGRemove Support

BGRemove removes image backgrounds locally on your Mac. Compare a cutout with its original, keep a transparent background or choose a new background, and export PNG images.

Contact

Email 3482208013@qq.com. Include your macOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. A screenshot can help, but avoid sending private images, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Images and processing

PNG, JPEG, BMP, and WebP images are supported. Background removal uses a model included in the app and does not require uploading images. Results vary with the image. Complex edges or low-contrast backgrounds may need manual refinement. The app requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 13.4 or later.

Free preview and paid export

Image import and background-removal previews are free. Export requires an active monthly or yearly subscription, or a lifetime purchase. Each paid plan unlocks unlimited image exports. Current prices are displayed in the app by the App Store.

Restore purchases

Use the same Apple Account used for your original purchase. If paid access is missing, use Restore Purchases in the app and follow Apple's prompts. An internet connection may be required. The restore button is hidden when the app already recognizes paid access.

Subscriptions and refunds

Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. For refund requests, visit Apple's refund website; Apple decides refund eligibility.
