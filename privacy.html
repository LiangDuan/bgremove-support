Privacy Policy

BGRemove · Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy describes how BGRemove handles information. For questions, contact 3482208013@qq.com.

Image processing stays on your Mac

BGRemove reads the images and background images you choose and processes them on your device using a bundled model. We do not upload your images, filenames, file paths, or background-removal results to a developer-operated server. Exported files are saved to the location you choose. The app does not require a BGRemove account.

Local temporary files

The app creates temporary result files for previews and editing. These remain on your Mac. Normal shutdown removes session temporary outputs; the app can also clean abandoned session outputs from previous runs. Exported images remain in your chosen folder until you delete them. The app does not delete your original images.

No advertising or analytics tracking

BGRemove does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking, and we do not collect app usage data through a developer-operated service.

Purchases are handled by Apple

Optional purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. BGRemove uses StoreKit to obtain product information and verify purchase entitlements on your device. We do not operate a purchase server or receive your payment card details. App Store transactions may require communication with Apple. Apple's handling of account, payment, and purchase information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and troubleshoot, do not sell it, and keep it only as needed to handle your request and any applicable legal obligations. Your email provider processes messages under its own policy. Do not send sensitive images or credentials. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any legally required retention.

This support website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and do not use analytics scripts, advertising, or contact forms. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Website hosting is separate from image processing in the app.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if the app's privacy practices change.

隐私政策

BGRemove · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 3 日

本政策说明 BGRemove 如何处理信息。如有疑问，请联系 3482208013@qq.com。

图片在你的 Mac 上处理

BGRemove 读取你选择的图片和背景图片，通过内置模型在设备本地处理。我们不会将图片、文件名、路径或抠图结果上传到开发者运营的服务器。导出文件保存到你选择的位置，App 不要求注册 BGRemove 账户。

本地临时文件

App 为预览和编辑生成临时结果文件，这些文件留在你的 Mac 上。正常退出会清理本次会话的临时结果，App 也可能清理此前运行留下的废弃会话结果。导出文件会保留在你选择的文件夹，直到你自行删除。App 不会删除原始图片。

没有广告或分析追踪

BGRemove 不包含广告、第三方分析 SDK 或跨 App 追踪，我们也不会通过开发者服务收集 App 使用数据。

购买由 Apple 处理

可选购买和订阅由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。BGRemove 使用 StoreKit 获取商品信息并在设备上验证购买权益。我们没有购买服务器，也不会收到你的银行卡信息。App Store 交易可能需要与 Apple 通信。Apple 对账户、付款和购买信息的处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。

联系支持时的信息

如果你主动发送邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和你选择提供的信息，用于回复与排查问题，不会出售这些信息，仅在处理请求或履行适用法律义务所需期间保存。邮件服务商按照其自身政策处理邮件。请勿发送敏感照片或凭证。你可以通过邮件请求删除支持通信记录，但依法必须保留的内容除外。

此支持网站

这些网页通过 GitHub Pages 托管，没有分析脚本、广告或联系表单。GitHub 可能为运营与保障服务安全处理 IP 地址等技术请求信息，详情见 GitHub 隐私声明。网页托管与 App 的本地图片处理彼此独立。

政策变更

如果 App 的隐私处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页及日期。
