SharpSnap AI Photo Enhancer Support

Enhance images locally on your Mac with bundled general, face and anime models. Compare the original and enhanced image, then export the results you want to keep. Images are not uploaded to the developer. No app account is required.

Contact

Email 3482208013@qq.com with your macOS version, app version and a description of the issue. Do not send private images, passwords, payment information or other sensitive content.

Models and requirements

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 13.4 or later. Import PNG, JPEG, BMP or WebP images. General Enhancement and Anime Enhancement produce 4× output dimensions. Face Restoration preserves the original dimensions and requires a usable detected face. Each model processes the original image, not another model's result. Processing is queued one image at a time.

Results depend on the source image. AI may alter or invent fine details and cannot guarantee accurate faces or text. The app limits 4× output to 100 megapixels. Processing time depends on image size and your Mac.

Compare and export

Use Side by Side, Slider or Overlay, zoom and pan to inspect results. Either side panel can be collapsed. Preview results are temporary and should be exported before quitting. Choose an export folder in Export Settings. If the destination is unavailable or not writable, the app asks you to select and authorize a folder in the system picker. Cancelling stops export. Results are exported as PNG without overwriting original images.

Free preview and paid export

Import and enhancement previews are free. Export requires an active monthly or yearly subscription, or a lifetime purchase. All three plans unlock exports for the available models. Current regional prices are supplied by the App Store and displayed in the app. View Plans remains available when paid access is active.

Restore purchases

Use the Apple Account used for the original purchase. If access is missing, choose Restore Purchases in the app and follow Apple's prompts. An internet connection may be required. Restore Purchases is shown when the app does not recognize active access. If prices fail to load, check your connection and choose Reload prices.

Subscriptions and refunds

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel them in your Apple Account subscription settings. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. For refund requests, visit Apple's refund website; Apple decides refund eligibility.
